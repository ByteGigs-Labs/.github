## Welcome

Thanks for stopping by. ByteGigs Labs is a team of builders who start with a problem people actually have, not a technology looking for a use. Most of our work begins in Kenya, with the people around us: health facilities losing money to rejected claims, freelancers whose payments get frozen, families targeted by M-PESA scams. We build the tool, test it properly, and share the code.

If you care about building things that matter, and building them well, you're welcome here.

## How we work

- **Security first.** Secrets never go in code, data is handled as if it were our own, and every project is built with the threat model in mind.
- **Real problems, real users.** We talk to the people a tool is for before we build it, and we design for the phone, the slow connection and the busy moment.
- **Explain, don't just decide.** Our tools show *why*: the rule, the evidence, the source. Users can check our work.
- **Tested and honest.** Code is reviewed and tested before it merges, and every project documents its limitations as clearly as its features.
- **Open by default.** Our projects are open source, usually under the MIT licence.

## Build with us

We welcome developers, designers, security researchers, technical writers and domain experts (health billing, payments, compliance). Students and early-career builders are welcome too. Everyone starts somewhere, and good reviews are how we all get better.

**Getting started**

1. **Pick a project** from the table above and read its README to run it locally.
2. **Find an issue.** Look for issues labelled `good first issue` or `help wanted`, or open one to suggest an improvement.
3. **Say hello first** for anything larger than a small fix, by commenting on the issue so we can agree on the approach before you write code.
4. **Open a pull request** from a branch in your fork, with a clear description of what changed and why.

**Our standards for contributions**

- One focused change per pull request, with tests for new behaviour.
- Clear, descriptive commit messages.
- All checks pass: tests, linting and dependency audits.
- No secrets, credentials or real personal data anywhere: use synthetic data for tests and demos.
- At least one review before merging. We review the code, never the person.

## Our community standards

We expect everyone to be respectful, patient and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people. Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated, and maintainers will remove contributions or contributors who don't follow these standards.

## Reporting a security issue

Please **do not** open a public issue for a security vulnerability. Email [info@bytegigslabs.com](mailto:info@bytegigslabs.com) with the details and steps to reproduce. We'll acknowledge your report and keep you updated while we fix it.
